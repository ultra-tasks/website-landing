Privacy policy,
plainly said.

What Ultra Tasks collects, what it does not, and what we cannot see. One policy covers this website, the apps you download, and the optional UltraSync sync service.

By Adrian Chase · Updated 8 November 2026

In short

  • The apps have no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your tasks stay on your device, and the apps need no account.
  • This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. That is the only analytics we run anywhere.
  • If you choose to create an UltraSync account, we store your email address and your encrypted account data. Nothing else personal.
  • We cannot view your tasks, or any data you choose to sync with us. It is encrypted on your device before it leaves, and we never hold the keys. That also means we cannot recover your login credentials, so keep your recovery key safe.
  • App stores, GitHub and the services you connect yourself have their own policies. We say where they come into play.

Who this covers

This policy applies to:

  • This website, ultratasks.app, including the download page and the guides.
  • The Ultra Tasks apps for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, and for iPhone, iPad and the web once they are released.
  • UltraSync, the optional sync service we run at app.ultratasks.app, including its account dashboard.

It does not cover servers run by other people (you can host UltraSync yourself, and whoever runs that server sets its policy), the stores you download from, GitHub, or services you connect to the app yourself. Ultra Tasks is independently maintained by Adrian Chase, who is responsible for the personal data described here.

This website

  • Visit counting. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. Your browser loads a small script from Cloudflare and reports each page view. Cloudflare says it uses no cookies or localStorage and does not fingerprint visitors for analytics. This is the only analytics we use.
  • Hosting. Cloudflare serves the site, so it handles each request for a page, including your IP address, as any web host does.
  • Downloads. A Download button opens an address on ultratasks.app that sends your browser on to GitHub. GitHub then serves the file, and its own privacy statement applies to that request.
  • Nothing else. The pages set no cookies, show no ads, have no sign-up forms, and load no outside fonts or scripts apart from the visit counter above. The Sign in link opens the UltraSync dashboard, which is covered below.

The apps

The apps have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. You do not need an account to use them. Your tasks, projects, notes and settings are stored on your own device, and we have no access to them.

What the apps may contact

  • An update check, on desktop only. The Windows, macOS and Linux apps ask GitHub for the number of the latest release shortly after they start and then once a day. The request carries no account, identifier or task data, but GitHub sees your IP address as it does for any web request. Turn it off in Settings (look for “Automatically check for new versions”). Builds from the Microsoft Store, the Mac App Store and the Snap Store skip the check because the store updates them. The Android, iPhone, iPad and web versions never make it.
  • Sync services you turn on. If you connect UltraSync, WebDAV or Nextcloud, Dropbox or OneDrive, your device talks to that service directly. For services other than ours, their privacy policies apply and we never receive the data.
  • Integrations and plugins you set up. They contact the service you point them at, such as an issue tracker or a calendar, using the details you enter. We are not a party to those connections.
  • App stores. If you install from a store, the store collects its own information about installs, updates and, depending on your settings, crashes, under its own privacy policy, and may share aggregate figures with us. We add no analytics of our own to the apps, and a store does not give us your tasks.
  • Android. The Standard Android build includes Google’s Play services libraries, and anything Google collects through them falls under Google’s privacy policy. The F-Droid build does not include them.

UltraSync accounts (optional)

You can use Ultra Tasks for as long as you like without an account. An account is only for UltraSync, our private sync service. UltraSync is in beta, and new sign-ups on the official service are closed for now. This section covers the accounts that exist and the ones that will open later.

What we store

  • Your email address, and a display name or username if you add one. We need it to create and verify the account and to help you recover access.
  • Your encrypted account data: your encrypted key vault, and the tasks and other data you sync, in encrypted form that we cannot read.
  • Sign-in and security details: a cryptographic record that lets the server check your password without ever receiving it, the details needed to check your second factor or passkey, and one-way hashes of your recovery codes.
  • Your devices: the name of each device you sign in from and when, so you can see and revoke them in the dashboard.
  • Account dates: when you created the account and when you accepted the terms and this privacy policy.
  • Technical data: the size and timing of your encrypted records and, as with any server, your IP address and the time of each request. We use it to run the service, limit request rates and block abuse. Server logs rotate automatically.

What we cannot see

We cannot view your tasks, nor any data you choose to sync with us.

Everything you sync, including tasks, projects, notes, tags and settings, is encrypted on your device before it is sent, with keys that are created and kept on your devices. The server only ever receives encrypted data. We do not have your keys, we keep no copy of them, and no support tool, backup or request could open your data for us. Your password and your recovery key are never sent to us in readable form.

We cannot recover your login credentials. We never receive your password or your recovery key in readable form, so we cannot look them up, send them to you, or reset them in a way that brings your data back. If you forget your password, use the recovery option on the sign-in page, which needs your recovery key (and your second factor, if you use one). If you lose both your password and your recovery key, we cannot restore your data, so keep your recovery key somewhere safe.

And if an authority ever asked us for your tasks, we could not provide them. All we hold is the list above.

What encryption does not hide

  • Your email address, your device and session details, the size and timing of your encrypted records, and the IP address you connect from are visible to the server.
  • Sync encryption does not protect the copy of your tasks that each of your devices keeps for itself. Use your device’s own disk encryption for that.
  • The account dashboard is a web page our server delivers to your browser, so signing in there means trusting the page we serve.
  • UltraSync is in beta and has not yet had an independent cryptographic review.

How we use it

Only to run your account: to let you sign in, to verify your email address, to keep your encrypted data available on your devices, to send you verification, recovery and security messages, to protect the service from abuse, and to tell you about important changes to it. We do not sell or rent your email address, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not analyse your data. We cannot read it.

Who else handles it

We run UltraSync on Oracle Cloud, which hosts the servers and database that hold the data listed above. Purelymail delivers the account emails we send you, such as verification, recovery and security messages, so it handles your email address and the content of those messages. Each handles that data only to provide its service to us. We share nothing else with anyone unless the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

  • We keep your account for as long as it exists, so your devices can keep syncing.
  • You can delete your account yourself from the account dashboard. That removes your account record, device sessions, second-factor details and encrypted data from the live service right away.
  • Backups kept for disaster recovery can hold older copies until they are replaced in the normal rotation. They contain the same kinds of data as above, and the encrypted part stays unreadable to us.

If you host it yourself

You can run your own UltraSync server. Your devices then talk only to your server and we receive nothing. Whoever runs that server is responsible for its data and sets its own policy.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The apps keep your data in their own storage on your device, which is how they work offline. The UltraSync dashboard keeps only what it needs in your browser to keep you signed in and to finish a login you started from the app. None of it is used for advertising or analytics.

Your choices and rights

Because the apps send us nothing, there is very little for us to hold about you. You can:

  • Delete your UltraSync account at any time, from the dashboard.
  • Turn off the desktop update check in Settings, or use the apps without ever connecting to anything.
  • Export your tasks from the app whenever you like. They are on your device already.
  • Ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in the EU and UK give you these rights. We will respond to such requests wherever you are, once we have verified that the request really comes from you. For an account, that usually means confirming through the email address on it, and we may decline a request we cannot verify.

If you are in the EU or UK and are unhappy with how we handled a request, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect or how we use it, we will say what changed on this page.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Email hi@ultratasks.app, or open an issue on GitHub. Please do not post personal details in a public issue: say that you have a privacy request and we will arrange a private reply, or email us instead. If the request is about an account, write from the address on it. We use what you send only to answer you.
